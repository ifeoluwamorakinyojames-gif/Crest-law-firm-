Editorial guardrails

Every page should give useful, answer-first explanations, cite official Nigerian legal sources where legal facts are discussed, identify an appropriate responsible lawyer and explain the firm's location and contact information accurately.

Legal content, credentials and advertising claims require Counsel's review. Avoid unverifiable success rates, claims of being the “best” lawyer or promises of results. Keep staging pages marked noindex until the official domain and publication are approved.
