How to contact the firm

Crest Crown Attorneys can be reached using the phone, WhatsApp and email contact methods published on its website. Please avoid sending confidential case details or documents in an initial message.

The current static website links visitors to WhatsApp or their own email application. It does not provide a confidential client portal, registration account or on-site document-upload form. If you decide to contact the firm through a third-party messaging service, that service may process information according to its own policies.

Website technology and external links

The website may load publicly available image, font and hosting resources and includes links to external services, such as maps and messaging applications. Those services are separate from the firm's website. The firm should verify its actual hosting, analytics and retention practices before approving this notice.

Requests about personal information

For questions about information you have shared with the firm, contact crestcrownattorneys@gmail.com. A final, firm-approved notice must specify the responsible data controller, purposes, lawful bases, retention, rights and complaint procedures, as applicable.
